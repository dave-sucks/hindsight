## Appendix — queries used

- Clean scorecard by era + env: `Position` JOIN `AgentConfig` ON `analystId`, `status='CLOSED'`,
  `closeReason NOT LIKE 'RECONCILE_DUPLICATE%' AND closeReason <> 'PROMOTED'`, grouped on
  `openedAt >= '2026-05-27'` and `environment`.
- Per-trade detail with `initialQty * avgCost` as entry notional → the sizing finding.
- Approvals from the **`Order`** table only: `side` × `intent` × `status`, `createdAt >= '2026-06-15'`.
  Never `ThesisUpdate PROPOSAL_*`.
- Expired/rejected BUY detail with `rejectionMessage` + `rationale` → the re-entry-tax table;
  marked to live Finnhub `/quote` (`cache: no-store` path, quotes taken 2026-08-20 ~15:50 ET).
- Watchlist staleness: `Thesis` JOIN `ResearchRun` ON `researchRunId` JOIN `AgentConfig` ON
  `agentConfigId`, `status IN ('WATCHING','HOLDING')`, aged on **`researchUpdatedAt`** — `updatedAt`
  is refreshed by daily REVIEWED audit rows and will make every thesis look 0 days old.
- Run-mode census: `ResearchRun` grouped by `mode` → the discovery-cron finding.
