## Bucket 2 — costs money

**1. IOT's floor was set inside one day's noise, and it fired.**
Dave set IOT's floor by hand at $41.40. IOT's ATR(14) is $2.29 on a $39.55
stock — 5.8% of price, the jumpiest name on the book. The floor sits 3.6%
under the $42.93 peak, so it is **tighter than 0.7 of one day's range**. It
fired at 09:30 this morning and a 230-share close is sitting in the queue.

This is the exact problem #683 was built for — "the larger of 3× the stock's
average daily range or the analyst's percent" — and #683 does not reach it,
because IOT's floor is a typed price level and #683 only widens a
`TRAILING_FROM_HIGH`. See the next section; this is the single most useful
thing this review found.

**2. SMMT: the floor said $17.40 and the book got $16.92.**
Closed +$1,157.94, +17.9% — a WIN, and the best trade on the book this month.
But the fill was 2.8% under the level that asked for it, $216 on 450 shares.
The sequence:

| | |
|---|---|
| Fri 10:25 | floor broke at ~$17.45, close proposed |
| Fri 16:00 | SMMT closed at **$17.85, +24.25%** — the break was a whipsaw |
| Mon 04:00 | that proposal expired without a decision |
| Mon 11:05 | floor broke again, close proposed |
| Mon 11:18 | approved |
| Mon 11:20 | filled at $16.92 |

Declining Friday was right — the stock recovered the same afternoon. The cost
sits in the shape of the floor, not the decision: a $17.40 line on an $18.73
peak is a 7.1% give-back on a biotech, which fires on noise once and on a real
break once, and there is no way to tell them apart at the moment it fires.

**3. Two buys the Secular Compounder wanted are now unblocked, and neither
will fire.**
ISRG (09-16, 09-17) and ETN (09-18) were refused at the 4-position limit. Dave
raised that analyst's cap to 6 at 11:55 today. But buys fire on the crossing,
and both crossings are spent: ETN closed Friday at $424.77 against a $418 buy
level, ISRG at $393.33 against $383. Both sit **above** their entries, so
nothing fires unless they dip back under and cross again. The capacity opened
after the crossings were used up. Either re-price the two entries or buy them
by hand — leaving them as they are is a decision to skip both.
