## Bucket 1 — breaks the loop

1. **A buy fired on Friday's price (invariant 6).** At the 09:30:25 pass the
   Finnhub quote hadn't rolled to Monday: price $425.41 (Friday's close),
   prior close $409.15 (Thursday's). The buy check tests "true now, false at
   the prior close", so it counted Friday's crossing a second time. ETN's
   high today is $404.86: it never traded above $418. The tactical run
   "confirmed" the same stale price and called `place_trade`; only the
   4-stock limit stopped it. The evaluator evaluates stale quotes on
   purpose, because skipping a stale *stop* is unsafe, but for a buy both
   numbers belong to yesterday. When it can happen: the one-day cooldown
   blocks most weekday repeats, because yesterday's fire was less than 24
   hours ago. That leaves the first check after a weekend or holiday, and
   crossings in a session's last minutes that no check caught. It's the
   first buy fire in the 09:30 window since crossing semantics shipped on
   09-02. The agreed fix: buys and adds don't fire on a quote more than 15
   minutes old; sells keep firing. **DAV-261.**

2. **Still live because #635 wasn't merged yet (it merged later that day).** Catalyst's summary again drops
   the SRRK sale it proposed ("account still reflects an open hold") after
