## What today's runs actually show that is wrong

### 1. Scale-in rungs fire on stocks we don't own (new, unfiled)

The account's standing rules include two ADD rungs: "price up 7% in a day —
scale in" and "price down 7% in a day — scale in". The cascade resolver drops
position-scoped **predicates** (`GAIN_FROM_ENTRY`, `TRAILING_FROM_HIGH`) on a
WATCHING thesis, but these two use `PRICE_MOVE_PCT`, so they pass, and
`effectiveTriggerAction` only remaps EXIT and upside REVIEW. Result: an ADD
fire on a watch row spawns a tactical run to "scale in" to a position that does
not exist.

Today: HPE 09:35, RARE 09:35, PLTR 09:55, NOW 10:15 — all WATCHING, all
spawned by ADD. Plus SMMT twice (held, so legitimate). **5 of 8 tactical runs.**
The HPE and RARE runs went on to retire both theses (WATCHING → RETIRED,
INVALIDATED). RARE fell 15% in a day so that may be right on the merits, but the
question the agent was asked was "add to your position", and it had none.

Fixed in this PR: the resolver drops ADD / TRIM / MOVE_STOP rungs on any
thesis that is not HELD, at every level. One rule, next to the predicate gate
that already existed.

### 2. Cooldown is lost when a thesis rung is dropped in favour of its inherited twin

SMMT above. Filed, not fixed here — the handoff in `dropRedundantInherited`
covers rungs that *restate* an inherited one and are pruned; it does not cover
a resend that omits the rung while the inherited twin still applies.

### 3. A breakeven stop on a held name is refused by the ordering rule

SMMT tactical runs hit `invalid_thesis_shape (LONG-stop-not-below-entry)` three
times and `protective_level_locked` twice today while trying to move the stop to
the $14.35 fill and then to $15.20. The rule `stop < entry` is right for a plan
we haven't bought; on a held name a stop at or above the fill is the whole point
of a ratchet. Already noted on #591; the fix is to exempt held rows from the
stop-vs-entry pair (ordering vs target still applies).

### 4. The narration→execution gap is still occurring

Yesterday's three daily runs produced 3 `complete_run` refusals: CRM and MU
"narrated close_position with no tool call". The gate held (nothing silently
skipped); the agent behaviour it guards against has not gone away.
