## Why the gate keeps rejecting — root cause hypothesis

Source: `lib/agent/system-prompts/intraday-tactical.ts:201-221`. The
prompt section "Confirmation gate before place_trade (DAY analysts
especially)" defines three sub-gates:

1. **Live quote still confirms the breakout** — legitimate; rejects ~3 of 30 (price faded back below trigger)
2. **Volume backs the move** — *"If today's volume is < 1.5x the 20-day average, the move lacks conviction"* — **dominant rejection reason: ~14 of 30 cite this verbatim, plus ~6 cite "no usable volume data returned"**
3. **No contradicting headline** — rejected ~2 of 30

**Two compounding problems with gate (2):**

**Problem A — Universal application of a DAY-only rule.** The prompt
qualifies the gate to *"DAY analysts especially"* and *"single-session
horizon"*, but the agent applies it identically to Tech Momentum (TRADE,
21-day max), Secular Theme (COMPOUNDER), EV Catalyst (CATALYST), and
Earnings Drift (TARGET, multi-week). A 1.5x daily-volume gate is a
sensible micro-filter for an intraday momentum chase. For an Earnings
Drift TARGET-horizon trade where the trader holds 1–12 weeks, today's
volume is essentially irrelevant — what matters is post-earnings drift
direction. The gate is rejecting Earnings Drift's AVGO ENTER for the same
reason it rejected Tech Momentum's WDAY.

**Problem B — Math is wrong shape for intraday timestamps.** The
comparison is `today's volume / 20-day-average-daily-volume`. At 09:45 ET
with 15 minutes of trading done, today's accumulated volume is
mechanically a small fraction of a full day's average — except for
exceptional names where it briefly isn't. Today's IMVT example:

- Time: 09:45 ET (15 min after open)
- Current volume: 1.17x the 20-day daily average
- Agent reads: "below the 1.5x threshold, lacks conviction, pass"
- Actual interpretation: **doing a full day's volume in 15 minutes is one of the strongest possible breakout signals**

Today's AVGO at 10:10 ET hit 0.19x — meaning ~19% of the daily average in
the first 40 min, which on a 6.5-hour session annualizes to ~1.85x daily
volume, ABOVE the gate. But the agent does the static comparison and
rejects.

WDAY at 12:25 AM ET shows the gate's worst failure mode: trading hasn't
started, current volume is the prior close's volume (or zero), and the
agent dutifully reports "0.89x average, below threshold" and passes.

**The fix is layered:**
- **Tactical prompt (Layer 2):** add a time-of-day correction — either
  (a) only apply the gate after 14:00 ET when the day is mostly done, or
  (b) compare current volume to time-of-day-adjusted historical average.
- **Tactical prompt (Layer 2):** make the gate apply only to TRADE-horizon
  and Intraday Momentum Scalper analysts. For TARGET / CATALYST /
  COMPOUNDER, drop the volume gate entirely and lean on the price-still-
  holding gate (#1).
- **Trigger evaluator (Layer 1):** don't fire ENTER tacticals between
  16:00 ET and 09:30 ET — they will mathematically fail gate (2) every time.
- **Don't write this entirely into the prompt.** Per `docs/PRINCIPLES.md`,
  if every analyst keeps tripping on the same instruction, the instruction
  belongs in tool semantics, not prompt text. Candidate: `get_stock_data`
  could expose `volumeRatioTimeOfDayAdjusted` so the agent doesn't have to
  reason about whether 09:45 ET volume is or isn't representative.

## New Findings

### F1. The 1.5x-volume gate, when applied to intraday triggers, mathematically rejects almost everything

The rationales for declined ENTER tacticals across 5/13–5/20 use the
phrase "volume confirmation" or "1.5x" 14 times out of 30 fires. An
additional 6 fires were declined because `get_stock_data` returned no
usable volume field at all ("no usable daily volume data," "the data
feed returned no usable daily"). The remaining 10 split between
price-faded-back (legitimate, ~3) and position-already-active or
size-cap (~5).

The volume gate as written in `intraday-tactical.ts:210-214` is doing
two failure modes at once:

1. **It applies to all horizons** even though the prompt comment scopes
   it to "DAY analysts especially / single-session horizon." Earnings
   Drift (TARGET, weeks-long) and EV Catalyst (CATALYST, event-driven)
   should not be subject to it; today they were.
2. **It assumes the agent will sensibly correct for time-of-day**, but
   the agent just does the static ratio. So 09:45 ET tacticals (when
   the day is 4% complete) get the same gate as 15:30 ET tacticals
   (94% complete).

**Worst illustrative case from today:** IMVT broke 52-week high at $32 at
09:45 ET with 1.17x of a full day's average volume already done in 15
minutes. The agent's own rationale called this *"strong IMVT-1402
clinical data... news is supportive."* And then rejected on the volume
ratio. If a 15-minute breakout to 117% of daily-average volume on a
positive clinical-data headline is not a buy for Catalyst Event Raider,
nothing is.

**Layered fix candidates** (do NOT just patch the prompt; see PRINCIPLES):
- **Tool layer (Layer 1):** add `volumeRatioTimeOfDayAdjusted` to
  `get_stock_data` output. Compute it as `currentVolume /
  expected_at_this_hour_of_20d_average`. The agent stops having to do
  the broken math.
- **Trigger evaluator (Layer 1):** suppress ENTER tactical fires outside
  US market hours. WDAY at 12:25 AM ET should never have spawned an
  agent run — it had no chance of confirming the gate.
- **Prompt (Layer 2):** make the volume gate conditional on horizon
  (apply only to TRADE + Intraday analysts) or drop it entirely and rely
  on the price-holding gate. The prompt's own "DAY analysts especially"
  qualifier should be made operative.

-- Morning runs today
SELECT r.id, ac.name AS analyst, ac."useV2Prompt", r.status,
       (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York') AS started_et,
       EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM (COALESCE(r."completedAt", NOW()) - r."startedAt"))::int AS elapsed_s,
       r.parameters->>'error' AS error_text,
       r.parameters->'toolStats'->>'totalToolCalls' AS total_tool_calls
FROM "ResearchRun" r
LEFT JOIN "AgentConfig" ac ON ac.id = r."agentConfigId"
WHERE r.mode = 'MORNING_PLAN'
  AND (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date = DATE '2026-05-20'
ORDER BY r."startedAt";

-- 14d trade-flow trajectory (the one that lit up the no-trade streak)
WITH morning_runs AS (
  SELECT r.id, (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date AS day
  FROM "ResearchRun" r
  WHERE r.mode = 'MORNING_PLAN'
    AND r."startedAt" >= NOW() - INTERVAL '21 days'
),
calls AS (
  SELECT mr.day, (regexp_matches(rm.content, '"type":"tool-call","toolCallId":"[^"]+","toolName":"([^"]+)"', 'g'))[1] AS tool
  FROM morning_runs mr JOIN "RunMessage" rm ON rm."runId" = mr.id
)
SELECT day,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE tool='place_trade') AS place_trade,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE tool='close_position') AS close_pos,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE tool='manage_position') AS manage_pos,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE tool='update_thesis') AS upd_thesis,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE tool='record_thesis') AS rec_thesis,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE tool='get_stock_data') AS stock_data
FROM calls GROUP BY day ORDER BY day DESC;

-- Position opens by day, last 21 days (the "last real open was 5/12" query)
SELECT (("openedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC') AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date AS day,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status::text != 'CANCELLED') AS opened_real,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status::text = 'CANCELLED') AS opened_cancelled
FROM "Position"
WHERE "openedAt" >= NOW() - INTERVAL '21 days'
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1 DESC;

-- ENTER tactical conversion (the 30-fire / 0-trade streak)
WITH enters AS (
  SELECT (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date AS day, COUNT(*) AS fires
  FROM "ResearchRun" r
  WHERE r.mode='INTRADAY_TACTICAL' AND r.parameters->>'action'='ENTER'
    AND r."startedAt" >= NOW() - INTERVAL '14 days'
  GROUP BY 1
),
pos_open AS (
  SELECT ("openedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date AS day,
         COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status::text != 'CANCELLED') AS opened
  FROM "Position" WHERE "openedAt" >= NOW() - INTERVAL '14 days' GROUP BY 1
)
SELECT COALESCE(e.day, p.day) AS day, COALESCE(e.fires,0) AS enter_fires, COALESCE(p.opened,0) AS pos_opened
FROM enters e FULL JOIN pos_open p ON e.day=p.day ORDER BY 1 DESC;

-- Rationale text on declined ENTER tacticals (proves the volume-gate pattern)
SELECT (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::timestamp(0) AS started_et,
       ac.name AS analyst, r.parameters->>'ticker' AS tk,
       LEFT(tu.rationale, 220) AS rationale
FROM "ResearchRun" r
JOIN "AgentConfig" ac ON ac.id = r."agentConfigId"
JOIN "ThesisUpdate" tu ON tu."runId" = r.id
JOIN "Thesis" t ON t.id = tu."thesisId"
WHERE r.mode='INTRADAY_TACTICAL' AND r.parameters->>'action'='ENTER'
  AND (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date BETWEEN DATE '2026-05-13' AND DATE '2026-05-20'
  AND t.ticker = r.parameters->>'ticker'
  AND tu.timestamp > r."startedAt"
ORDER BY started_et DESC;

-- Current ACTIVE positions (the "5 of 7 from April" view)
SELECT ac.name AS analyst, t.ticker, t.direction,
       t."entryPrice", t."targetPrice", t."stopLoss",
       (t."createdAt")::date AS opened,
       (NOW()::date - t."createdAt"::date) AS days_held
FROM "Thesis" t
JOIN "ResearchRun" rr ON rr.id = t."researchRunId"
JOIN "AgentConfig" ac ON ac.id = rr."agentConfigId"
WHERE t.status::text = 'ACTIVE' AND ac.enabled = true
ORDER BY t."createdAt";

-- Focus ratio per analyst today
WITH today_morning AS (
  SELECT r.id, r."agentConfigId" FROM "ResearchRun" r
  WHERE r.mode='MORNING_PLAN'
    AND (r."startedAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date = DATE '2026-05-20'
    AND r.status='COMPLETE'
),
touched AS (
  SELECT DISTINCT tu."thesisId", tm."agentConfigId"
  FROM "ThesisUpdate" tu JOIN today_morning tm ON tm.id = tu."runId"
),
books AS (
  SELECT ac.id, ac.name, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE t.status::text IN ('ACTIVE','WATCHING')) AS book
  FROM "AgentConfig" ac
  LEFT JOIN "ResearchRun" rr ON rr."agentConfigId" = ac.id
  LEFT JOIN "Thesis" t ON t."researchRunId" = rr.id
  WHERE ac.enabled=true GROUP BY ac.id, ac.name
)
SELECT b.name, b.book, COALESCE(COUNT(DISTINCT t."thesisId"), 0) AS touched,
       ROUND(100.0 * COALESCE(COUNT(DISTINCT t."thesisId"), 0) / NULLIF(b.book, 0), 1) AS focus_pct
FROM books b LEFT JOIN touched t ON t."agentConfigId" = b.id
GROUP BY b.name, b.book ORDER BY b.name;

-- Overdue-review backlog
SELECT
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status::text IN ('ACTIVE','WATCHING')) AS active_or_watching,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status::text IN ('ACTIVE','WATCHING')
                   AND "nextReviewAt" < (NOW() - INTERVAL '7 days')) AS overdue_7d,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE status::text IN ('ACTIVE','WATCHING')
                   AND "nextReviewAt" < NOW()) AS overdue_any
FROM "Thesis";
