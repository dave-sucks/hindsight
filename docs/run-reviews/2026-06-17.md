reached Alpaca.** Mix of price-monitor trailing-stop re-fires (`"Automated STOP

morning/tactical re-proposals. The gate + dedup are doing their job (no cost

### [Concern #5 — ISRG] Sizing anomaly confirmed
**ISRG (Secular, PAPER) = 34 sh @ $416.82 = $14,172** vs the rest of Secular's

analyst's paper P&L attribution.

### [F] Paper sells reached Alpaca with no approval audit — verify the paper gate
Momentum's 4 paper closes (SMTC, MTSI, MRVL, LRCX) reached **FILLED with
`alpacaOrderId` set and `closeSource = "reconcile"`, but there is no
`PROPOSAL_APPROVED` audit row for any of them** — despite `requireApprovalSellsPaper
= true`. Contrast the LIVE side, which gated perfectly. Two readings: (a) paper
sells are intentionally ungated and the toggle is cosmetic for paper, or (b) the
agent/tactical paper-close path bypasses the proposal seam and the reconcile cron
syncs the broker fill after the fact. **The LIVE gate is unaffected** — but this
should be confirmed in code before we trust the paper toggle as a faithful
rehearsal of the live flow.

### [F] Reconcile lag → orphan dup proposals (paper-only)
6/16's paper EXIT orders (MTSI, SMTC) filled at Alpaca on 6/16 but the DB
`Position` rows stayed **OPEN until the reconcile cron closed them ~09:30 on 6/17**
(~17h lag). The 6/17 morning run, reading them as still-held at 08:05, **re-proposed
sells** → two `AWAITING_APPROVAL` orders now stranded on already-CLOSED positions.
No money impact, but it's state desync + UI noise, and it inflates the proposal
queue with un-actionable rows. The fix is closing the DB position synchronously on
the paper fill (or a tighter reconcile cadence), not patching the morning run.

### [Observation] Live sells were SELLs, so P1-25 (orphan-on-decline) didn't bite
All three blocked live items (MU/PACS/CRDO) are sells, which are immune to the
P1-25 premature-flip bug (the zombie-gate refuses agent status-flips on
ACTIVE-with-open-position). Today's two live buys were *approved*, not rejected, so
no buy-side orphan was created. P1-25 remains untested in the wild — watch the next
*rejected/expired live buy*.
