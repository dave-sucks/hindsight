### How this was read

- **Watched stocks:** `Thesis` rows with status WATCHING on the trading account; the analyst is
  `Thesis → researchRun.agentConfigId`.
- **Buy trigger:** an ENTER in `Thesis.triggers`. A "near the 50-day" or "above the 50-day" buy
  was priced from the newest `TickerIndicators` snapshot (the 09-25 session).
- **Floor, target, average day's move:** `Thesis.stopLoss`, `Thesis.targetPrice`, and
  `snapshot.atr14` from the same snapshot.
- **Score:** `scoring.composite` against `minConfidence ÷ 10` (Catalyst 5, PEAD 6,
  Compounder 7). This is the comparison `place_trade` makes.
- **Slots:** OPEN plus PENDING_APPROVAL positions per analyst against `maxOpenPositions`. This
  is also the count `place_trade` uses.
- **Prices:** Alpaca's IEX last trade at 13:05 ET.
- **Cash:** the LIVE account in `SyncHealthSnapshot` at 13:01 ET.

where it looks first rather than a requirement, and raise it from 6 names to 8.

- **Why not a new analyst:** [`ANALYST_LINEUP.md`](../plans/ANALYST_LINEUP.md) settled on three
  analysts, with the Compounder as the quality, long-hold seat. Its research found that's the
  kind of holding that doesn't move with PEAD. The theme list turned it into a thematic seat
  (AI, power, medtech, defense) instead.
- **Why a fourth analyst doesn't pass the doc's bar:** a separate "quality stocks on
  pullbacks" analyst would use the Compounder's exact setups. The doc only allows a fourth
  analyst if it earns returns a different way, and this doesn't.
- **It isn't Momentum coming back:** Momentum picked stocks by price strength, which is the
  same bet PEAD makes. The Compounder picks the business and uses the chart only for timing.

**Correction to the brief:** the Compounder's five "no room" refusals (09-11 to 09-18) all hit
the old 4-slot setting, not its "3–4 names" sentence. Each `GateRejection` reads "at its
4-slot cap". Since the setting went to 6, one Compounder buy has fired (PLTR, 09-25), and the
analyst did try to buy it; that attempt was refused on size, which runs no longer set. The
sentence hasn't blocked a buy yet, but it contradicts the setting, so the edit changes it.
