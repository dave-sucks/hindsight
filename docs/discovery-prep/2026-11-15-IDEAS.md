## Ideas

**PEAD**

1. **Re-scan last month's season.** Every clean beat-and-raise from Oct 15 to Nov 13 is past
   its 1–3 day window; the best are pulling back toward a rising 20- or 50-day, which
   `MA_PULLBACK` can buy. Works in-app today: PEAD's screen pool is the earnings calendar.
   Paste below.
2. **The last wave of reports.** Watch wide before the print, buy narrow after it.
3. ⚠️ **The NVDA read-through.** Suppliers that gap up on volume the next day can be an
   `EPISODIC_PIVOT`.
4. **Under-covered mid-caps.** ZETA, PACS and CRDO were $2–15B names. Drift is strongest where
   few analysts cover the stock: aim at $1–10B with eight or fewer covering analysts.
5. **Price-target raises within 72 hours of the report** (3+ firms). No data plan carries this:
   MarketBeat, or the headline accounts in the roster.
6. **Guidance in management's own words** (call quotes) to tell a raised guide from a
   reiterated one, PEAD's #1 false positive.
7. **Gap-ups worth a look.** Finviz each evening: gap up over 8%, relative volume over 3,
   market cap over $1B.

**Catalyst**

8. **Calendar first.** Ask the Catalyst chat for every FDA decision in Jan–Feb 2027 inside its
   fence, marked against what it already watches, before any outside sourcing
   (the Oct 2 lesson).
9. **Already-approved drugs with an FDA date ahead** (label expansions, new uses): the shape
   that won. 70 to 21 days before the date puts January dates in the window now.
10. **ASH.** Hematology names with good abstract data and the full presentation still ahead.
    Check that `PRE_CATALYST` accepts a conference date, not only an FDA one.
11. **JPM run-up.** Biotech and medtech tend to rise through December into it.
12. **Russell December rebalance.** Health-care and tech names joining or moving up.
13. **S&P 500 adds.**
14. **Nasdaq-100 reshuffle.**

**Smart money**

15. **Q3 13F convergence.** Names three or more long-term funds started or added to. The
    playbook already treats agreement among multi-year holders as real signal for the
    Compounder.
16. **Insider cluster buys** (OpenInsider): 3+ insiders within a week, $100k+ each, after a
    drop. Best as conviction on names already watched.
17. **Government contracts and Congress trades.** Evidence on a stock, not a copy-trade:
    members can report up to 45 days late (roadmap 10.2).

**Compounder**

18. **Leaders, not cheap quality.** The live losers were big quality names bought on dips that
    kept falling. Look at stocks within 10% of their high and above their 50- and 200-day,
    bought on the first pullback.
19. ⚠️ **The next layer of AI build-out** (power, cooling, optical, memory).
20. ⚠️ **Software that's speeding up** (net new ARR acceleration).

**Seasonal**

21. **Tax-loss rebound.** Stocks down 40%+ this year that still had a good recent report get
    sold into late Nov–Dec and often bounce in early January. Watch now; buy on a close back
    above the 50-day.

## Pastes

**PEAD chat (idea 1):**
```
List every company in your fence that reported between Oct 15 and Nov 13 with an EPS beat,
a revenue beat and raised full-year guidance (use run_screen PEAD and the earnings calendar
back 30 days). For each, say how much it gapped on the print and where its rising 20-day and
50-day averages are now. Dispatch up to 5 that clear, as pullback theses with a buy at the
rising average, a floor and a target. Wait for each writer, then end with one table:
stock · buy · floor · target · distance from the buy.
```

**Grok, earnings scouts:**
```
Who on X has called post-earnings drift correctly in 2026, meaning names that kept rising
for weeks after a beat-and-raise? Give me 10–15 people, what they called, and whether they
posted this month. Then: which names that reported since Oct 15 are those people still
bullish on, and what exactly was raised? Label each with market cap and its gap on the
print. Don't filter.
```

**Grok, fund filings:**
```
Q3 2026 13F filings are due Nov 16. Which US-listed stocks did 3 or more long-only,
multi-year funds (not quant or pod shops) start or add to significantly in Q3? For each:
the funds, the size of the add, and anything the fund has said publicly. Label sector and
market cap. Don't filter.
```

**Grok, biotech (future events only):**
```
Which US-listed biotechs between $1B and $20B present at ASH (Dec 12–15, 2026) where the
abstract already showed positive data, or have an FDA decision in Jan–Feb 2027 on a drug
already approved for something else? For each: the event, the date, and where the date
comes from. Mark unverified dates "unverified" instead of dropping the row.
```

**Grok, scout scorecard:**
```
For @handle: list the specific stock calls they made between July and September 2026, with
the date and price, and what each stock did after. Count hits and misses, and don't count
calls made after the move.
```
